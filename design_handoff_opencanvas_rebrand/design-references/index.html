Brand & Design System

The new Open Canvas
looks like a tool you'd actually enjoy.

A warmer, friendlier rebrand of the site builder — built for people who think in Word & Docs, not code. Crisp black canvas frames, one confident coral red, and a heading face with a little character. Here's the system, then the three screens it dresses.

How I read the brief

assumptions & direction — tell me where I'm off
audience

Comfortable, not technical

Small-business owners & marketers who can drag-and-drop but don't care what's under the hood. Zero jargon, big tap targets, plain-language labels.

feeling

Warm & a little playful

Rounded surfaces, soft warm shadows, friendly copy, gentle motion — in the Framer / Gamma family. Confident, never clinical or "terminal-y."

brand

The frame that breaks open

Your logo's red bars punch across the canvas frame. I made that the recurring device: overshooting underlines, active states, the live cursor.

scope

One system, every surface

Same tokens dress the landing page, the dashboard, and the editor. Light primary, full dark mode. Every existing feature keeps its place.
