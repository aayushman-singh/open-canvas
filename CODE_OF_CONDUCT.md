# Code of Conduct

Open Canvas adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as the standard for behaviour in all project spaces (this repo, issues, PRs, and any official discussion channel). The full text is at the link above and is incorporated here by reference.

## In short

- Be respectful and constructive in disagreement.
- Assume good faith; ask before reacting.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not tolerated.
- Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or ban contributors who violate the standard.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to **kremzylo@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer, kept confidential, and acknowledged within 72 hours.

## Scope

Applies to anyone interacting in project spaces or representing the project in public — including issues, pull requests, code review, and any official social channel.
